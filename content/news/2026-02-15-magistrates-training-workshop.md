## Magistrates & Police Officers Training Workshop

The CJF Secretariat, in partnership with UNICEF Zambia, conducted a two-day capacity-building workshop for magistrates and police officers across the Lusaka and Copperbelt provinces.

### Workshop Objectives

- Strengthen participants' understanding of the Children's Code Act
- Build skills in child-sensitive interviewing and court proceedings
- Introduce updated ARRS protocols for police stations
- Review diversion eligibility criteria and referral pathways

### Participants

Over 45 magistrates, 60 police officers, and 20 social welfare officers attended across the two days.

### Key Outcomes

Participants completed the workshop with updated knowledge of child justice procedures and received official certificates of completion from the Judiciary of Zambia.
