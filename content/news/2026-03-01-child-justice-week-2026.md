## Child Justice Week 2026

Child Justice Week is the CJF's flagship annual campaign, held every March across all nine provinces of Zambia. The campaign raises public awareness of children's rights within the justice system and celebrates progress made in child justice reform.

### This Year's Activities

- **Community outreach** in all 9 provinces led by Local Child Justice Forums
- **School visits** by magistrates and police officers to educate students on their rights
- **Media campaign** across national radio and television
- **Stakeholder forum** in Lusaka bringing together all CJF member institutions
- **Open Day** at the Magistrates Court Complex for the public

### Theme

*"Every Child Deserves Justice — Protect, Rehabilitate, Reintegrate"*

For more information, contact the CJF Secretariat at CJF-Z@judiciaryzambia.com.
