History

Established May 2001

Born from a landmark situational analysis that exposed critical gaps in how Zambia's justice system treated children.

2001
CJF Founded

Established under the Judiciary of Zambia as a multi-sectoral platform for child justice reform.

2018
Diversion Programme Launched

Piloted in Lusaka, Ndola, Kitwe, and Kapiri Mposhi with UNICEF support.

Today
40+ Forums Nationwide

Active across all 10 provinces with support from UNICEF, GIZ, CIDRZ, and Save the Children.

Who We Are

Coordinating Justice for Every Child Since 2001

The Child Justice Forum is an open-ended consortium of key institutions in Zambia's justice system — police, magistrates, social welfare, legal aid, and civil society — working together so that every child is treated with fairness, dignity, and care.

⚖️ Coordination 🛡️ Protection 🌱 Rehabilitation 📢 Advocacy

What We Do

Three Core Programs

Changing how Zambia's justice system works for children.

🔄

Diversion Programs

Steering children away from formal court and criminal records toward community-based rehabilitation and family reintegration.

Learn more → 🏛️

Child Friendly Courts

Redesigning court environments and procedures so children are heard, understood, and not re-traumatised by the process.

Learn more → 🤝

ARRS

Arrest Reception Referral Services — ensuring every child gets immediate, appropriate support at first contact with police.
